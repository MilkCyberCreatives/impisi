# Impisi Resources - SEO Account Setup Checklist

These are off-site accounts that cannot be created from source code alone and require owner/admin access.

## Priority 1

1. Google Search Console
- Add property for the production domain.
- Verify with DNS TXT or the `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` value in app metadata.
- Submit `https://<your-domain>/sitemap.xml`.

2. Google Business Profile (Google My Business)
- Claim or create the profile for Impisi Resources.
- Keep Name, Address, Phone, website URL, and business category consistent with the site.
- Add business description, services, operating hours, and logo/cover photos.
- Complete verification (phone, email, video, or postcard depending on eligibility).

3. Google Analytics 4
- Create property and web data stream for the production domain.
- Link GA4 with Search Console and Google Ads (if used).

## Priority 2

1. Bing Webmaster Tools
- Add site and submit `sitemap.xml`.

2. LinkedIn Company Page
- Ensure website URL and business details match site and Google Business Profile.

3. Social profile consistency
- Finalize Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp links used in the site header/footer.

## Local SEO Hygiene

1. Keep business name/phone/email exactly consistent across all listings.
2. Use a single canonical domain (with or without `www`) across every account.
3. Publish updates and images regularly in Google Business Profile.
4. Monitor Search Console coverage/indexing weekly.
